A family business that kept growing.

It started in 1985 with fuel deliveries to homes and institutions in the Bekaa. Customers trusted us, and the business grew with them.

Today Tahan Group also produces, blends and fills lubricating oils for Lebanon, Egypt and Iraq, and manufactures the PET and HDPE containers they are sold in.

  1. 1985

    Fuel distribution

    Tahan Petroleum starts delivering fuel across the Bekaa.

  2. Then

    Lubricant plant

    Production, blending, filling and re-refining of oils.

  3. Then

    Plastics factory

    PET and HDPE containers in many shapes and sizes.

  4. Next

    Africa

    Our lubricants are heading to new markets.
